## Scene 2 — Introducing our application — 0:18–0:35 — Ria
LTO Officer is our mobile-based traffic violation ticketing system, designed to help officers manage traffic tickets digitally.

We developed our application to make the ticketing process more organized, convenient, and efficient.

## Scene 3 — Officer login and dashboard — 0:35–0:48 — Ria
Our application provides an officer login system and a dashboard that brings essential ticketing features together in one place.

With a simple interface, officers can easily navigate through the application.

## Scene 4 — Creating a traffic ticket — 0:48–1:08 — Micah
Creating a traffic ticket is more convenient with our application.

Officers can enter the violator's information, select an address, and record vehicle details through guided steps.

We also included input validation to help minimize encoding errors.

## Scene 5 — Capturing evidence — 1:08–1:25 — Micah
To support proper documentation, we integrated a camera feature that allows officers to capture photo evidence directly within the application.

We also included location functionality, allowing GPS information to be attached when available.

## Scene 6 — Violations and fines — 1:25–1:45 — Joseph
One of the key features we developed is our violation selection system.

Officers can select the applicable traffic violations, and our application automatically calculates the corresponding total fines.

This reduces manual computation during ticket preparation.

## Scene 7 — Digital traffic ticket — 1:45–2:00 — Joseph
After completing the required information, our application generates a digital traffic violation ticket.

Each ticket includes a unique reference number and QR code to support organized record management.

## Scene 8 — Offline capability — 2:00–2:18 — Joseph
We also designed our application with offline-first functionality.

Traffic ticket records can be saved locally when internet connectivity is unavailable and synchronized with the server once the connection is restored.

## Scene 9 — Our purpose — 2:18–2:35 — Ria
As the developers of LTO Officer, we wanted to create a practical digital solution that supports traffic officers in their daily responsibilities.

Through teamwork and innovation, we transformed our ideas into a working mobile application.
