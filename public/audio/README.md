# Audio sources and rights

preview-mix.mp3 is prepared from the team's cleaned Ria recording and the original
procedural sound effects generated by scripts/prepare-audio.py. The effects use
only mathematical oscillators and seeded noise; no downloaded samples or reference
video audio are used. They may be used with this project and its exported videos
without attribution. No third-party musical composition is included.

No licensed music track was supplied. src/audio/music.json is a disabled slot,
not an empty or fake audio file. To use it, supply audio/music.mp3 and a licenseNote
that documents permission for this video, then enable and regenerate the mix.
The pipeline includes sidechain ducking, a low bed level, and gentle end fades.

Prepared future effects include shutter, confirmation, notification and sync.
Only clock ticks, transitional whooshes, small clicks, a logo rise and a quiet
phone-reveal confirmation are active in the current 20-second preview.
